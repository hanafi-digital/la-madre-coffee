Filosofi & Dedikasi

EXTRACTION BEYOND EXPECTATION

"From the first sip, we invite you to experience coffee at its highest standard."

Our coffees are roasted with uncompromising precision, strictly aligned with the international benchmarks of the Specialty Coffee Association (SCA). Each lot is then assessed and curated by SCA CVA Cuppers, ensuring that only the finest potential of every bean is brought to life. Every cup reflects not only consistency and quality but also the pursuit of excellence.

Di La Madre Karawang, setiap batch biji kopi disangrai mandiri menggunakan profil sangrai terkalibrasi. Dipadukan dengan atmosfer kedai yang tenang dan lega, kami menyediakan ruang kerja santai (WFC Sanctuary) dengan Wi-Fi kencang, colokan di setiap meja, serta area indoor AC sejuk bebas asap rokok dan outdoor smoking terrace asri.
